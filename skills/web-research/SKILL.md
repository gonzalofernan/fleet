# Web Research

- Prefer primary and authoritative sources.
- Record source URLs, publication dates when available, and access dates.
- Separate observed claims from interpretation and recommendations.
- Call out uncertainty, conflicting sources, and stale information.
- Do not log in, publish, purchase, send, or modify external data without authorization.
- Deliver a concise report with an answer, evidence, assumptions, and open questions.
